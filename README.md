# Het-PDP-T_Instances
Repository with Het-PDP-T instances.

The instances are based on the following publication: Ghilas, V., Cordeau, J.F., Demir, E., van Woensel, T., 2018. Branch-and-price for the pickup and delivery problem with time windows and scheduled lines. Transportation Science 52, 1191–1210. [doi:10.1287/trsc.2017.0798](https://doi.org/10.1287/trsc.2017.0798)

The original instances can be downloaded here (last checked on 04.01.2026): [https://tomvanwoensel.com/instances-used/](https://tomvanwoensel.com/instances-used/)

**GhilasA** are the original PDPTW-T instances of Ghilas et al. 2018 rewritten in a new format. 

**GhilasB** and **GhilasSL_PDPT** are new modified benchmarks, where we do not consider a vehicle limit, adapted the request loads to [1,Q_max] and fixed time windows for our model.
**GhilasB** are based on the PDPTW-T instances of Ghilas et al. 2018 and **GhilasSL_PDPT** are based on the PDPTW-SL instances of Ghilas et al. 2018.
